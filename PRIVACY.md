# Privacy Policy for IppoIppo

*Last updated: November 21, 2026*

IppoIppo does not collect, store or share any personal data.

All habits and check-ins you create are stored only on your device. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not send any data to the developer or to third parties.

If you use iCloud or device backups, your app data may be included in those backups according to your own Apple settings. The developer has no access to them.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
